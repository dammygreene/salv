# CULLER - Launch Plan

## Launch position
Launch `$CULLER` on Solana.

The product can scan supported assets across multiple chains, but Solana is the native home of the token and first-class product experience.

## Launch venue
Preferred venue: StonkFun, subject to verifying its current official launch configuration, creator fee mechanics, trading mechanics, and any applicable constraints at launch time.

Do not hardcode third-party-reported fee rates into product copy.

If StonkFun no longer fits the intended simple fee-to-treasury model, evaluate other Solana-native launch venues based on:
- transparent creator economics
- reliable liquidity
- safe program architecture
- straightforward ownership controls
- ability to transparently route project fees to treasury
- low operational complexity

## Token launch principles
- No private investor round.
- No strategic allocation.
- No separate marketing bucket.
- No fake partnership claims.
- Public supply and allocation from day one.
- Community rewards are finite.
- Treasury wallet and relevant program addresses should be public when safe.

## Launch content
The first message should sell the product, not the token.

Example:
`YOUR WALLET IS FULL OF DEAD ASSETS.`

`CULLER finds them, helps you remove them, and rewards the cleanup.`

Then introduce `$CULLER` as the reward token.

## Launch sequence
1. Publish product demo.
2. Open waitlist or early access if useful.
3. Release public scan.
4. Verify cull flow.
5. Publish reward rules.
6. Launch token.
7. Activate first reward epoch.
8. Begin treasury fee accounting.
9. Publish cull statistics.

## Important operational separation
Keep treasury, reward distribution, and deployment authority in separate addresses where appropriate.

Use multisig for administrative treasury controls when possible.

## Public transparency page
Show:
- supply
- allocations
- reward epoch status
- community rewards emitted
- treasury balance where appropriate
- buyback transactions when they occur
- contract addresses

Do not present treasury balances as guaranteed future buybacks.
